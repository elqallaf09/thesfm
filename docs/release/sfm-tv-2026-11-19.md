# SFM TV — one-screen selection and remote usability

## Scope

Owner request: make Save and view reachable by remote, keep market customization
inside one TV screen, remove the duplicate lower Market hours entry, move My
channels to the upper controls with readable labels, and show headlines together
instead of a one-item news carousel.

Base: `1945131821fac99c60122e957c14292891b85723`.
Android preview: version `1.0.20`, build `26`.

- Embedded session selection has its own focus scope and Back destination. All
  81 clock exchanges fit the 1280×720 and 1920×1080 acceptance layouts, with
  compact names, flags, selection indicators and a fixed Save action.
- The market customizer uses a compact directory and fixed editing controls.
  Arrow keys follow visible rows, OK opens the market controls, and Back returns
  to the chosen market before closing its dialog.
- Upper actions have visible localized names; the footer no longer repeats
  Market hours or My channels.
- Received headlines render together with source/time and the existing full
  story detail on OK. The general feed remains capped by the server at eight;
  no price, licensing, authentication, RLS or provider behavior changes.
- Android Back recognizes embedded editors as well as dialogs.
