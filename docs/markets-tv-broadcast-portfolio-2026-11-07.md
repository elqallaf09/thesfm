# SFM TV: broadcast layout and private portfolio

World market hours previously showed three cards across 27 pages while the overview sidebar pushed news below the TV viewport. The full 81-exchange selection now uses five balanced pages (17/17/17/17/13), advances every 40 seconds, and supports pause, next/previous, saved subsets, and select-all. Scheduled-open cards use the positive theme color. Local clocks and second-by-second opening/closing countdowns remain visible; selecting a card opens its complete schedule, Kuwait target time, and source QR. The existing reference limits for holidays, Ramadan, and exceptional closures still apply.

News, scene movers, and the overview session summary have separate bounded panels. Their pages rotate automatically rather than requiring sidebar scrolling. Font and row measurements follow the allocated panel, including panels mounted after a network response. Overnight chart labels include the changed date. Long navigation labels and quote evidence fit alongside the additional portfolio tab. Existing invalid-measurement ticker recovery remains covered.

## My investments

Portfolio behavior updated on 2026-11-20: the TV view reads the same `investment_items` records and normalization as the website's Investments Center every 15 seconds, with recovery after visibility/network changes. The private response can overlay verified market valuations without modifying the saved records. Cards keep the asset, platform, value and valuation status visible; remote OK opens the complete source, quantity, cost and time details, and Back restores card focus. Twelve positions use two columns without position pagination; larger portfolios remain reachable through contained scrolling. No portfolio values are placed in persistent browser storage or the shared public snapshot cache.

The server resolves saved listing identity, quantity and pricing units before using existing quote services. Physical metals require verified weight/purity; USD and USDT remain distinct. The response retains the quote source, observation time and delayed/closed/reference status, and keeps the previous recorded value, source and time available in details. Unverified identities, missing quantities/units or unavailable quotes retain the recorded valuation with an explicit reason. Manually valued assets such as property retain their recorded valuation and date. Bounded quote requests and public quote caches do not contain account holdings.

Currency conversion requires a verified, sufficiently recent rate, with its own source and observation time. If conversion is unavailable, the native-currency market value stays visible and is excluded from the selected-currency total. Summary and allocation labels distinguish market, recorded and mixed values; capital coverage is separate, so subtracting incomplete capital from the valuation total is not presented as portfolio profit. “Portfolio fetched” records the response time, not a new price observation or a streaming entitlement.

`GET /api/tv/portfolio` requires an active, unexpired paired-device token. The owner is resolved server-side; every investment page and profile query is constrained to that owner. Client-supplied owner selectors are rejected. Responses are private/no-store and project only display fields, excluding notes, AI analysis, snapshots, and user identifiers. Missing/revoked authorization clears displayed values and open position details. Pairing and unlink copy includes investments. The market overlay is read-only: no database migration, investment write, quantity change or purchase-cost rewrite is introduced.
