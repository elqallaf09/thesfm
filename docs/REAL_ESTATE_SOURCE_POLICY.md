# Real Estate Intelligence — Source Policy

## Bosnia and Herzegovina (Federation of BiH)
The Federal Administration for Geodetic and Real Property Affairs (FGU) operates the Real Estate Price Register (RCN) with the Tax Administration and local-government units. Official market reports are based on registered purchase contracts and are suitable as government report-level evidence when the report contains the geography/property metrics required by the analyst.

Production rule: do not bulk-copy or commercially republish protected cadastral/RCN public-view records. FGU publicly warned in 2026 about unauthorized downloading, combining and commercial use of RCN/cadastral data. THE SFM should use published reports, permitted public data, or an authorized/licensed integration.

Official references:
- https://katastar.ba/rcn
- https://www.fgu.com.ba/en/newse-reader/real-estate-market-report-of-the-federation-of-bih-for-year-2025.html

## Turkey
TKGM provides official Web Tapu and parcel-query services and supports official property/declaration-value context. This establishes a high-authority identity/context source for land/property records.

Production rule: do not infer that TKGM exposes an unrestricted parcel-level completed-sale-price feed. Until an authorized/open transaction-price source is implemented, TKGM evidence is registry/report/statistical context only. A valuation still requires defensible comparable price evidence from a permitted source.

Official references:
- https://www.tkgm.gov.tr/
- https://www.tkgm.gov.tr/en/node/6485

## Global rule
A country is not shown as fully valuation-supported merely because an official cadastral website exists. Coverage should distinguish:
1. parcel/identity coverage,
2. official market/report coverage,
3. comparable transaction coverage,
4. FX coverage,
5. valuation-ready coverage.

If any required layer is missing, the UI must say what is unavailable instead of fabricating a current value.
