# Economic Intelligence Decision Engine

The first shared decision model intentionally starts with deterministic financial safety checks before adding AI-generated explanations.

## Supported decisions

- Buy a car
- Buy a home
- Take a loan
- Pay down debt versus invest
- Start a business

## Initial signals

- Monthly surplus after the proposed decision
- Liquidity runway after upfront cash use
- Projected debt-service pressure
- Source data completeness

## Output contract

The engine returns a risk score, an affordability band, reasons, and warnings. These outputs are decision-support signals. They are not promises, predictions of guaranteed outcomes, or regulated financial advice.

## AI boundary

AI may later explain the deterministic result, compare scenarios, and summarize relevant economic context. AI must not replace the arithmetic engine or fabricate missing balances, income, expenses, debts, or investments.
