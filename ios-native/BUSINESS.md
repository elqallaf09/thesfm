# THE SFM Business for iPhone

The `TheSFMBusiness` target in `TheSFM.xcodeproj` is a separate iPhone application with bundle identifier `com.thesfm.business` and scheme `TheSFMBusiness`.

It establishes native workspace navigation for projects, customers, and invoices. The initial screen does not display synthetic business data; each live area awaits its own protected API contract and device QA.
