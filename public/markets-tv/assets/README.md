# Bundled TV asset images

Gold and silver: existing repository assets from public/images/metals.

Company logos: downloaded on 2026-11-07 from the FMP image service:
https://financialmodelingprep.com/image-stock/{SYMBOL}.png

Bundled symbols: TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, ASML.AS, SAP.DE, NESN.SW, 7203.T, 6758.T, 0700.HK.

Company trademarks remain owned by their respective companies. Used to identify the corresponding security; matching is constrained by listing/exchange. No logo implies sponsorship.

The market atlas pairs an illustrated Kuwait waterfront generated with Higgsfield with a separate geographic layer. The latter uses the public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m land and land-boundary data in a Robinson projection centred on Greenwich. The 2:1 map extent is −17,400,000 to 17,400,000 m east and −7,800,000 to 9,600,000 m north. The interactive city positions use the same projection and extent.

SFM TV also bundles the Ganesh Housing mark (NSE:GANESHHOU, ISIN INE460C01014)
from [the NSE logo collection](https://github.com/shareperks/indian-listed-company-logos/blob/main/nse/NSE_GANESHHOU.svg)
and the white Volatility Shares mark for SVIX from [NVSTly](https://github.com/nvstly/icons/blob/main/ticker_icons/SVIX.png).

The six index images are the instrument marks published on TradingView's SP:SPX,
DJ:DJI, NASDAQ:NDX, FTSE:UKX, XETR:DAX and TVC:NI225 pages.
SFM supplies its own text badges when these images are unavailable.
Shanghai marks are matched to their SSE instrument pages (600370, 603359,
603378) and [JD Capital](https://www.jdcapital.com/en) (600053).
Fallback image requests to FMP retain the .SS or .NS exchange suffix.
